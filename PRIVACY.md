# Privacy Policy: Exact Dates for YouTube

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Exact Dates for YouTube does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

**What the extension does.** It runs only on youtube.com. It reads the "... ago" labels on the pages you view and replaces them with each video's real publish date. To find that date, it asks YouTube itself for the video's details, the same way the YouTube page does.

**What is stored.** Your chosen display mode (ON, BOTH or OFF) and a cache of video publish dates are saved locally in your browser, so each video only needs to be looked up once. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

**What is not done.** No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies or ads.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's page or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
